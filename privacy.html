Privacy Policy

SRE Bot, operated by Skyroute Entertainment

Effective Date: September 20, 2026
Service: SRE Bot
Operator: Skyroute Entertainment

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Skyroute Entertainment collects, uses, stores, and processes information through the SRE Bot (also known as "Secretary"), a Discord bot used within Skyroute Entertainment's Discord server and related operations.

By interacting with the SRE Bot, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The SRE Bot processes information necessary to perform verification, application processing, administration, activity monitoring, and moderation functions.

Discord Information

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server, channel, role, and message information
  • Message content in configured verification channels
  • Reactions and interaction information
  • Voice-channel state information
  • Application and verification messages
  • Relevant timestamps

Roblox Information

When a Roblox username is submitted for verification, the SRE Bot may send that username to Roblox services and retrieve publicly available account information.

  • Roblox username
  • Roblox display name
  • Roblox user ID
  • Account creation date and calculated account age
  • Avatar/headshot URL
  • Follower count
  • Following count
  • Inventory-related verification information
  • A derived security/email-verification status

The SRE Bot does not retrieve or store a user's actual Roblox email address. The bot may query Roblox inventory information and derive a status such as email verified, email unverified, or private inventory.

Application Information

Information submitted through the company's application and verification process may include:

  • Roblox username
  • Age
  • Company
  • Position
  • Inviter information
  • Discord username and Discord user ID
  • Application message content
  • Verification and activity timestamps
  • Member activity and status information

3. How Information Is Used

Skyroute Entertainment uses the information processed by the SRE Bot for operational purposes, including:

  • Verifying Roblox accounts
  • Checking Roblox account eligibility
  • Processing company applications
  • Recording Step 3 verification information
  • Assigning or updating Discord roles
  • Synchronizing Discord nicknames with Roblox usernames
  • Managing company ownership and activity status
  • Monitoring required member and owner activity
  • Sending verification and activity notifications
  • Maintaining administrative and audit logs
  • Moderating voice-channel and soundboard activity
  • Responding to authorized administrative commands

4. Roblox Verification

During Step 1 verification, the SRE Bot may send a submitted Roblox username to Roblox APIs to determine whether the account exists and to retrieve information such as the account's profile, avatar, follower count, following count, creation date, and inventory-related status.

The bot calculates the account's age and checks whether it meets the configured minimum requirement of 445 days.

Retrieved verification information may be summarized in Discord audit messages. The bot does not directly store a user's Roblox email address, IP address, or cookies.

5. Google Sheets

Step 3 verification records are stored in Google Sheets through the Google Sheets API.

These records may include:

  • Timestamp
  • Discord username
  • Discord user ID
  • Roblox username
  • Applicant age
  • Position
  • Verification status
  • Inviter username or tag
  • Inviter Discord ID
  • Last activity or DM-ping timestamp
  • Activity deadline information

The bot can append, read, and update relevant spreadsheet records as required by its verification and activity systems. The bot does not implement a user-data deletion workflow for these records.

6. Discord Storage and Logs

The SRE Bot may create or maintain Discord messages containing verification results, audit information, moderation alerts, activity notices, soundboard logs, application information, and automated notifications.

The bot does not delete application, audit, log, or notification messages as part of its normal operation, although it may remove or clear interactive buttons from certain direct messages.

Retention of Discord messages is also affected by Discord and server configuration and cannot be determined solely from the bot's code.

7. Local Configuration and Security

The SRE Bot uses local configuration files for operational information such as Discord configuration, company information, spreadsheet IDs, company owner IDs, invite links, and activity-related values.

Authentication credentials such as the Discord bot token and Google service-account credentials are intended to remain private and are not part of the publicly accessible policy website.

The SRE Bot does not intentionally display or publish these credentials.

8. Voice and Soundboard Information

The SRE Bot may process voice-channel state information for moderation purposes.

The bot may also record soundboard-related events, including information such as the Discord user, user ID, voice channel, timestamp, and sound involved.

Certain configured soundboard activity may result in temporary voice restrictions or disconnection from a voice channel.

9. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Based on the current implementation of the SRE Bot, the bot does not directly collect or store:

  • Users' actual email addresses
  • IP addresses
  • Cookies
  • Discord attachments as user-data records
  • User-uploaded files through the bot

Third-party services such as Discord, Roblox, Google, or hosting infrastructure may independently process information according to their own policies and systems.

10. Third-Party Services

The SRE Bot interacts with external services required for its operation, including:

  • Discord and Discord APIs
  • Roblox Users APIs
  • Roblox Thumbnails APIs
  • Roblox Friends APIs
  • Roblox Inventory APIs
  • Google Sheets API

Information sent to these services is subject to the respective third-party service's own privacy practices and policies.

11. Data Retention

The SRE Bot does not define a single universal retention period for all information it processes.

Information may remain in Discord messages, Google Sheets, local configuration, or other operational records until it is manually changed, removed, or otherwise affected by the relevant service's retention controls.

The exact retention periods of Discord, Roblox, Google, hosting providers, or other external services are not determined by the SRE Bot's code.

12. Data Deletion

The current SRE Bot implementation does not provide a user-facing command for deleting personal information from its verification records.

Users who have questions regarding information processed by the SRE Bot may contact Skyroute Entertainment using the contact information below.
