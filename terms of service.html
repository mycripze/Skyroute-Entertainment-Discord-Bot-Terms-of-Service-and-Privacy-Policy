3. Roblox Verification

The SRE Bot may verify a Roblox account submitted by a user as part of the server's verification process.

Step 1 verification checks information obtained through Roblox services, including account existence, profile information, avatar information, follower and following counts, account creation date, and inventory-related information.

A Roblox account must be at least 445 days old to meet the configured Step 1 account-age requirement.

Verification results may affect whether the user proceeds through the server's verification process.

4. Company Applications

Step 2

Step 2 involves processing company information and obtaining approval from the configured owner of the relevant company.

Company ownership and approval information may be checked by the SRE Bot before the application proceeds.

Step 3

Step 3 requires the applicant to provide the information requested by the verification system, which may include Roblox username, age, company, position, and inviter information.

The configured Step 3 age range is 16 to 120. Submitted information may be validated and recorded in the company's configured Google Sheet.

8. Voice and Soundboard Moderation

The SRE Bot may monitor soundboard activity in configured voice environments for moderation purposes.

The system tracks soundboard events within its configured moderation window. Excessive soundboard use may result in temporary voice restrictions or disconnection from a voice channel.

Under the current configuration, five soundboard events within ten seconds may trigger a three-minute voice restriction. The bot may disconnect the member and prevent rejoining during the restriction period.

These restrictions apply to the configured soundboard moderation system and do not generally restrict ordinary text-channel communication.
